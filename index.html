Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Watchio include channels, movies, or subscriptions?

No. Watchio is a player. You connect your own lawful provider or playlist.

Can I install Watchio now?

Not yet. Public APK links and Downloader codes will appear here when release builds are ready.

Which providers work with Watchio?

Watchio supports compatible Xtream Codes and M3U providers, plus XMLTV / EPG data where available.

Can I control it with a TV remote?

Yes. Android TV and Fire TV remote navigation and focus handling are core parts of the app.

Will Watchio support Windows?

Windows remains a future target. Current development focuses on Android, Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

Player only. Your content.

Watchio is intended for lawful playback of user-provided content. Watchio does not host, sell, or provide IPTV subscriptions, channels, movies, series, or unauthorized copyrighted streams.
